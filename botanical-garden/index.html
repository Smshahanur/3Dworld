Horticultural Features & Architecture

Designed with realistic glass transmission shaders, biophilic architecture, and animated natural elements.

🌱

Geodesic Glass Conservatory

An arched glass-and-steel greenhouse featuring high-transmittance physical materials that let soft directional sunlight bathe the indoor tropical palm groves and fern clusters.

🦋

Fluttering Butterfly Swarms

Procedural butterfly particles fluttering around the blooming azaleas and orchids with wing-flapping vertex displacement and gentle hovering physics.

🖊

Lotus & Lily Water Basins

Calm reflective pools covered in blooming water lilies and broad pads, reflecting surrounding weeping willows and sky lighting.

🌶

Medicinal & Herb Arboreta

Carefully curated rows of historical medicinal herbs, lavender bushes, and aromatic flora complete with informational botanical signs for curious virtual botanists.
