3DWorld – Explore an Interactive 3D Virtual World

Explore 3DWorld, an interactive 3D virtual world featuring floating islands, crystal landscapes, futuristic architecture and immersive environments.

Explore 3DWorld

Step into a living, real-time 3D universe built with WebGL and Three.js. Navigate across an ancient floating archipelago in the sky, orbit majestic landmarks, cycle atmospheric dawn-to-night lighting, and listen to synthesized ambient soundscapes directly in your web browser.

Floating Island Landmarks

Crystal Valley

Ancient subterranean geodes and towering resonant amethyst spires projecting harmonic frequencies across the floating isle.

Sky Temple

The central celestial landmark featuring layered rotating rings, glowing arcane runes, crystal fragments, and radiant energy portals connecting dimensional planes.

Aurora Forest

Bioluminescent elder canopies, ethereal flora, ground bushes, and drifting starlight particles illuminating the island's western ridge.

Cloud City

Anti-gravity futuristic towers, observation citadels, architectural sky bridges, and glowing windows suspended high above the cloud sea.

About 3DWorld

3DWorld is an interactive virtual-world showcase engineered for discovering imaginative environments directly in modern web browsers. Featuring real procedural geometry, physical PBR materials, responsive camera controls, and zero external asset dependencies.
