3DWorld – Explore an Interactive 3D Virtual World

Explore 3DWorld, an interactive 3D virtual world featuring floating islands, crystal landscapes, futuristic architecture and immersive environments.

Explore 3DWorld

Step into a living, real-time 3D universe built with WebGL and Three.js. Navigate across an ancient floating archipelago in the sky, orbit majestic landmarks, cycle atmospheric dawn-to-night lighting, and listen to synthesized ambient soundscapes directly in your web browser.

Modern Village Districts

Town Center

Pedestrian civic plaza, modern library atrium with clock tower, outdoor café terraces, bakery, boutique shops, and tree-lined avenues.

Residential District

Contemporary two-story villas and multi-unit residences with landscaped front gardens, glass balconies, driveways, and quiet tree-lined avenues.

Central Park

Grand public parkland featuring a tiered ornamental water fountain, timber gazebo pavilion, children's adventure playground, and walking promenades.

Botanical Garden

Glass-paned conservatory greenhouse, geometric parterre flower displays, ornamental boxwood topiary hedges, and water lily reflection ponds.

Lake District

Reflective freshwater lake bordered by wooden promenades, an arched footbridge, waterfront picnic glades, weeping willows, and scenic hills.
