Explore 3DWorld

Step into a living, real-time 3D virtual universe built with WebGL and Three.js. Travel across connected districts including Rural Village, Modern Village, and Capital Town, drive electric vehicles, interact with resident AI characters, cycle atmospheric dawn-to-night lighting, and explore with friends in multiplayer directly in your web browser.

Modern Village Districts

Town Center

Pedestrian civic plaza, modern library atrium with clock tower, outdoor café terraces, bakery, boutique shops, and tree-lined avenues.

Residential District

Contemporary two-story villas and multi-unit residences with landscaped front gardens, glass balconies, driveways, and quiet tree-lined avenues.

Central Park

Grand public parkland featuring a tiered ornamental water fountain, timber gazebo pavilion, children's adventure playground, and walking promenades.

Botanical Garden

Glass-paned conservatory greenhouse, geometric parterre flower displays, ornamental boxwood topiary hedges, and water lily reflection ponds.

Lake District

Reflective freshwater lake bordered by wooden promenades, an arched footbridge, waterfront picnic glades, weeping willows, and scenic hills.

Explore the 3DWorld Districts

Discover all seven public regions and architectural zones across 3DWorld. Each area features distinct topography, interactive landmarks, resident NPCs, and realistic environmental physics:

  • Rural Village Guide – Explore traditional pastoral farmland, organic wheat crops, and mud-stucco thatched cottages on the western plains.
  • Modern Village Guide – Discover contemporary sustainable villas, the bustling town center plaza, boutique shopping, and artisan espresso cafes.
  • Capital Town Guide – Tour the metropolitan skyline, high-rise commercial center, central transit terminal, and civic municipal plaza.
  • Central Park Guide – Stroll through manicured lawns, the ornamental tiered water fountain, classical gazebo pavilion, and jogging promenade loops.
  • Botanical Garden Guide – Step inside the geodesic glass conservatory greenhouse, exotic floral parterres, and animated butterfly swarms.
  • Lake District Guide – Walk the illuminated wooden waterfront boardwalk, arched river footbridges, and scenic lake promenades.
  • About the Creator – Shahanur Mollah – Learn about the creator and developer of 3DWorld, technical WebGL architecture, and the 3D Creator Pavilion.
