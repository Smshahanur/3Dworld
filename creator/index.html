Technical Architecture & Philosophy

How Shahanur Mollah engineered 3DWorld from the ground up.

⚡

WebGL & Three.js Engine

Custom shaders for water caustics, volumetric day/night lighting, real-time shadow cascades, and optimized draw-call batching for smooth 60fps across mobile and desktop.

🚗

EV Driving Dynamics

Rigid-body wheel friction physics, Ackermann steering kinematics, regenerative braking deceleration, and interactive AI traffic navigation.

🌐

Persistent Multiplayer

Real-time player position syncing via Firebase Firestore and WebSockets with dead reckoning interpolation, custom chat channels, and interactive emotes.
