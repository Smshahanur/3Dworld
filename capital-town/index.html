Capital Town CBD in 3DWorld

The metropolitan epicenter of 3DWorld, Capital Town is a high-density urban district defined by sleek glass skyscrapers, the monumental neoclassical City Hall, the Central Transit Terminal, and broad four-lane boulevards.

Explore Capital Town in 3D →

Skyline Architecture & Financial Towers

Rising high above the eastern plateau, Capital Town features the multi-tier Capital Financial Tower, modern glass-curtain high-rises, and the Grand Galleria department store. Night illumination bathes the towers in cybernetic indigo and neon accents.

  • Location Coordinates X: 110, Z: 0
  • Elevation Metropolitan Core • 16m
  • Atmosphere Indigo Glow & Corporate Neon
  • Speed Limit 60 km/h (Express Highway)

Grand Municipal Hall & Obelisk Court

At the center of Capital Civic Plaza stands the Grand Municipal City Hall, fronted by Corinthian-inspired columns and a golden clock tower. Across the promenade, an illuminated obelisk and granite water basin provide a regal public gathering space before the boulevard connects west toward Central Park and the Lake District.

  • Civic Center Grand Municipal City Hall
  • Monument Grand Civic Obelisk & Water Basin
  • Transit Depot Central Transit Terminal (Multi-Bay)
  • Highway Connection Bridge 2 connecting to Modern Village

Executive & Urban Daily NPC Routines

Intelligent office workers, transit commuters, and civic leaders navigate the metropolitan core:

Director Sterling Cole: Commutes through the Grand Boulevard to City Hall for morning municipal planning hearings.

Architect Zaha Chen: Examines blueprints and urban structures around the Grand Galleria and high-rise plaza.

Arthur Bell: Manages daily transit schedules at the Central Transit Terminal connecting buses to the Rural Village loop.

Victor Moreau: Welcomes international delegates and residents at the Grand Obelisk Court.

High-Capacity Highway & Roundabouts

Capital Town's transportation network is designed for high capacity, featuring a four-lane divided boulevard, dedicated turning bays, traffic roundabouts, and elevated overpasses. Vehicles yield safely for pedestrians at all striped zebra crosswalks.

Drivers can access the high-speed Grand Capital Highway to travel west back to Modern Village, cross the river to visit the Rural Village farmlands, or tour the dedicated 3D pavilion built by Shahanur Mollah.
