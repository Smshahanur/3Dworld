Central Park in 3DWorld

The civic breathing room of the virtual universe. Unwind beside the multi-tiered fountain, rest in classical gazebos, jog along curved pebble tracks, and experience outdoor recreation connecting the Modern Village to Capital Town.

Park Attractions & Highlights

Every corner of Central Park is designed with classical landscaping, real-time particle effects, and dynamic visitor pedestrian paths.

💧

Grand Tiered Fountain

Standing at the center of the geometric esplanade, the triple-tier fountain features continuous water jet particle streams and light-refracting surface ripples rendered with custom WebGL shaders.

🏛

Classical Gazebo Pavilion

A hexagonal octastyle pavilion crafted with white columns and zinc domed roofing. Park visitors stop here to enjoy panoramic views across the botanical parterres and the distant skyline.

🌿

Boxwood Parterres & Flora

Symmetrical evergreen hedges framing seasonal floral beds in shades of lavender, geranium, and marigold, bordered by decorative limestone curbs and ambient night lanterns.

🏃

Recreation & Jogging Loops

Gravel running trails weaving throughout the park perimeter. Watch fitness-minded NPCs complete morning jogging routines or run alongside them in real time.

Life in the Park: Dynamic Characters

NPCs with specialized behavioral states give the grounds natural community presence.

🎶

Leo the Acoustic Musician

Frequently seen resting near the gazebo steps tuning his acoustic instrument, greeting passing park walkers and adding relaxed ambiance to the grounds.

🐶

Joggers & Morning Strollers

Local residents exercising along the outer ring road before commuting over the suspension bridge into Capital Town's commercial district.

📚

Leisure Readers

Visitors sitting on shaded cedar benches along the gravel promenade, enjoying the tranquil fountain sounds away from city vehicle traffic.
