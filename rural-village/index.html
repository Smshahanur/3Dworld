Rural Village in 3DWorld

Nestled on the western plains across the stone river bridge, the Rural Village in 3DWorld preserves traditional pastoral life with mud-stucco thatched cottages, tilled agricultural farmlands, timber grain mills, and close-knit community gatherings.

Explore Rural Village in 3D →

Pastoral Farmland & Organic Crops

The western sector of Rural Village is dedicated to sustainable crop cultivation. Golden wheat bushels, sunflower rows, and vegetable beds are maintained along natural soil furrows. Local agricultural practices provide fresh flour, wildflower honeycomb, and organic produce to the entire virtual universe.

  • Location Coordinates X: -110, Z: 0
  • Elevation Pastoral Valley • 8m
  • Atmosphere Warm Amber & Sunflower Gold
  • Speed Limit 30 km/h (Pedestrian Priority)

Community Architecture & Landmarks

Unlike the steel high-rises of Capital Town, Rural Village architecture embraces earthen materials, timber beams, and natural thatch roofs. Key landmarks include the ancient village spring well, the primary community school, and the village tea stall beneath a spreading banyan tree.

  • Primary Landmarks Grain Mill, Spring Well, School
  • Housing Style Mud-Stucco Thatched Cottages
  • River Crossing Bridge 1 connecting to Lake District
  • Public Transport Inter-district Rural Bus Loop

Resident NPCs & Daily Routines

Rural Village features intelligent NPCs operating on realistic 24-hour daily schedules:

Farmer Liam: Tends to the golden wheat crops each morning and delivers flour bushels to the general farm stand by midday.

Farmer Nora: Manages the sunflower patch and beehives, harvesting honeycomb in the afternoon sun.

Grandpa Alistair: Sits peacefully outside on his shaded front veranda bench, greeting neighbors and travelers arriving from the Modern Village.

Young Maya: Walks along the village green toward the primary school before spending afternoons exploring the western meadows.

Pedestrian & EV Roadway Integration

The rural roads are engineered with gentle unpaved earth shoulders and stone paving near the center well. AI vehicles, including the inter-district bus and visiting sedans, respect pedestrian crosswalks and yield with a 3.5m stopping buffer whenever farmers or school children step out.

Visitors driving electric vehicles from the Modern Village can cross Bridge 1 over the crystal river to enter the scenic rural cul-de-sacs, or continue along the riverbank toward the Lake District waterfront. World architecture and road physics were developed by Shahanur Mollah.
