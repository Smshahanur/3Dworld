Modern Village in 3DWorld

The civic and community heart of 3DWorld, Modern Village combines Scandinavian-inspired residential villas, pedestrian-first brick plazas, alfresco cafes, sustainable electric mobility, and verdant public gardens.

Explore Modern Village in 3D →

Town Center Plaza & Alfresco Cafes

The Town Center is paved with interlocking granite and clay pavers. Visitors can relax at the Terrace Cafe, pick up fresh sourdough at the village bakery, or explore boutique design galleries framed by native birch trees and warm street lamps.

  • Location Coordinates X: -14, Z: 0
  • Elevation Street Level • 12m
  • Atmosphere Cyan Radiance & Timber Warmth
  • Speed Limit 40 km/h (Boulevard)

Contemporary Residential Villas

North of the boulevard lies the Residential District: modern two-story architectural homes featuring natural cedar slats, dark metal fascia, glass-railing balconies, private driveways, and sustainable green roof gardens with direct walking access to Central Park.

  • Neighborhood Style Modern Sustainable Villas
  • EV Infrastructure Public Fast Chargers & EV Bays
  • Green Spaces Direct Access to Central Park
  • Creator Pavilion Creator Studio (East Boulevard)

Intelligent Daily Life & NPCs

Modern Village bustles with lifelike citizen routines:

Barista Chloe: Serves artisan espresso and fresh pastries on the shaded terrace throughout morning and midday hours.

Alex Turner: Enjoys morning jogs through the boulevard and Central Park perimeter before visiting the cafe.

Sophie Lin: Strolls through the boutique shopping colonnade and rests on the Lake District boardwalk in the afternoon.

Dr. Aris Vance: Researches local flora between Modern Village and the adjacent Botanical Garden conservatory.

Highway Bridges & Inter-District Mobility

Modern Village serves as the central crossroads of the universe:

Head west across Bridge 1 (Rural West River Bridge) to discover pastoral farmlands in the Rural Village, or head east over Bridge 2 (Grand Capital Highway Bridge) to enter the bustling skyline of Capital Town. Stop by the Creator Pavilion at coordinates (27.5, 2.0, 15.0) to explore the development studio built by Shahanur Mollah.
